🐦 Common Songbirds of the United States

A guide to 19 beautiful backyard birds you might encounter

Bird Species Image Key Facts
American Robin Common garden bird with distinctive red-orange breast. Known for their cheerful warbling song. Often seen hopping on lawns searching for earthworms.
Northern Cardinal Brilliant red plumage in males, tan in females. Iconic crested head. Year-round resident in most of eastern North America. Distinctive "cheer-cheer-cheer" call.
Blue Jay Bright blue and white with distinctive crest. Known for their loud, raucous calls and intelligence. Acorn hoarders that help plant oak trees.
American Goldfinch Bright yellow and black males in breeding season. Active songbird attracted to nyjer seed feeders. Unique undulating flight pattern with "potato-chip" calls.
Carolina Chickadee Small gray bird with black cap and bib. Easily identified by "chick-a-dee-dee-dee" call. Regular backyard feeder visitor. Quick and acrobatic.
Tufted Titmouse Gray bird with peachy-rust flanks and distinctive crest. Loud "peter-peter-peter" song. Similar size and behavior to chickadees. Year-round resident.
House Finch Males have red plumage on head and breast. Energetic songbird with cheerful warbling. Native to western US, now common across North America.
Song Sparrow Streaky brown sparrow with distinctive central breast spot. Loud, melodious song. Found in brushy areas and gardens throughout North America.
Eastern Bluebird Brilliant blue and rusty-orange plumage. Medium-sized thrush with cheerful warbling. Uses nest boxes. Insectivore, benefits from open habitats.
Northern Mockingbird Gray and white bird with white wing patches visible in flight. Extraordinary mimic of other bird songs. Often sings at night. Territorial and aggressive.
Wood Thrush Russet-brown thrush with spotted breast. Beautiful flute-like song, one of the finest in North America. Forest and woodland dweller. Migratory.
Cedar Waxwing Sleek gray and yellow bird with distinctive black mask and red waxy wing tips. Highly social, often seen in flocks. Feeds on berries.
Indigo Bunting Males are brilliant blue, females tan-brown. Sweet, rapid warbling song. Common summer breeder in brushy, open habitats. Migratory.
Common Yellowthroat Small warbler with distinctive black mask on yellow face (males). Recognizable "witchity-witchity-witchity" song. Prefers wet, marshy habitats.
Yellow Warbler Bright yellow throughout, males have reddish streaks on breast. Sweet warbling song. Summer breeder in willows and shrubby areas near water.
Scarlet Tanager Breeding males are brilliant scarlet with black wings. Females and fall birds are yellow-green. Migratory forest dweller with distinctive song.
Common Grackle Large blackbird with iridescent plumage and distinctive harsh calls. Highly adaptable omnivore. Often seen in flocks, sometimes in huge numbers.
European Starling Dark iridescent bird with speckled plumage, yellow beak. Highly vocal and social, often in large flocks. Excellent mimics. Invasive species in North America.
Black-capped Chickadee Gray bird with black cap and white cheeks. Distinctive "chick-a-dee-dee-dee" call. Year-round resident in northern US. Regular feeder visitor. Remarkably intelligent.

PART ONE

The Foundations

I

The Meaning of Words

A word carries three layers at once: the concrete thing it names (a dog is a four-legged animal), the context that bends it (an idiom like "sly dog" means something else entirely), and the vast, practically unreachable record of every way that word has ever been used across the training data. Humans rely on all three without noticing. AI reads all three, deliberately, at the same time.

Written words carry far more than meets the eye.

II

Generative AI

A prompt becomes tokens — roughly 100,000 of them cover the English language. The model doesn't know the next word; it calculates the relative frequency of every token that could follow, weighs the likely candidates, and selects one. A short prompt can take on the order of 100 trillion computations to answer. The same brute-force statistics that make a response feel fluent are what occasionally sends it off the rails.

Coherence and hallucination come from the same machine.

III

RAG

Left alone, a model searches nearly its entire corpus for every answer — most of it irrelevant to what's actually being asked. Retrieval-Augmented Generation lets a specific, complete prompt act as a filter: it narrows the search, pulls a small set of real sources, and returns something that can be cited instead of invented.

A better-built prompt gives RAG something to filter.

THE MECHANISM

Why a Complete Prompt Matters

The Foundations aren't background material — they're the reason the Writing Coach works at all. Every review cycle is, underneath, an exercise in giving RAG less to search and more to find.

PART TWO

The Writing Coach

The writing coach does not write your prompt for you. It uses Claude to assess the prompt you've already drafted — naming what's working and what's still missing — so you stay the author while the gaps get surfaced.

STEP 1

Write the Narrative

Rough is fine. It just has to carry your real intent onto the page.

STEP 2

Copy for Review

One click adds a quiet instruction — review and advise — and sends it to Claude with the coach's role attached.

STEP 3

Claude Assesses

Not a rewrite — a diagnosis. What's working, what's missing, what decision you haven't made yet.

STEP 4

Revise & Repeat

Take what's useful, push back on the rest, run it again until the prompt is complete.
